About Us

Our story

Two coasts, one oven

From the heart of the Middle East to the soul of Tanzania.

Oriental Bakery is a Middle Eastern bakery on Mombasa Round in Stone Town, Zanzibar. We bake khubz, baklava, maamoul, croissants and artisan loaves fresh every day, using the methods they are made with back home.

Stone Town has always been a place where the Gulf, the Swahili coast and the Indian Ocean meet on the same street. Our bread belongs to that tradition — Middle Eastern baking, made daily on a Zanzibari corner.

What we stand for

Baked daily, sold the same day

  1. Fresh every morning

    Everything is baked on the day it is sold. When something comes out of the oven, it goes straight to the counter.

  2. Made the traditional way

    Khubz, baklava and maamoul are made the way they are made in the Middle East, not adapted down for convenience.

  3. Order however suits you

    Walk in, message us on WhatsApp, pre-order for a collection time, or set up a standing order. No app, no account.

The shop

Come and see the counter

We are on Mombasa Round in Stone Town. Look for the long wooden counter and the shelves of bread by the door.
